How a stub works.

Three choices at launch on StonkFun. The rest runs on chain.

01

Pair it with a stock

On StonkFun, pick a tokenized stock as your coin's pair: TSLAx, NVDAx, SPYx, AAPLx and more.

02

Make it a reward coin

Choose the reward fee model. Every trade carries a small transfer tax that's paid to holders, not to the dev.

03

Holders get the stock

The tax is paid out to holders pro-rata in the coin's pair. Hold the meme, collect the stock.

Questions.

What is stub?

stub is a way to find and launch memecoins that pay their holders in stocks. It uses StonkFun's reward coins: a coin paired with a tokenized stock pays a share of every trade to its holders in that stock.

Are the coins on this page real?

Yes. Every coin in the list is read live from StonkFun's public API: reward coins whose pair is a tokenized stock. Market cap and volume are StonkFun's numbers.

How much do holders get?

It depends on the coin's tax and its trading volume. Each card shows the coin's tax when StonkFun reports it. More trading means more paid out.

Is stub part of StonkFun?

No. stub is independent and built on StonkFun. It isn't affiliated with StonkFun or with the issuers of the tokenized stocks.
